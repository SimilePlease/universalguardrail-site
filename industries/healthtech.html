AI Security for HealthTech

Ensure absolute HIPAA compliance and patient safety when integrating generative LLMs into healthcare portals.

Evolving Threat Landscape

Healthcare technology requires absolute precision. Generative AI offers immense value in summarizing patient histories, automating clinical coding, and answering patient queries. However, the risk of exposing Protected Health Information (PHI) under HIPAA, or an AI hallucinating a medical diagnosis, carries potentially fatal consequences. Universal Guardrail is designed as a zero-trust intermediary for medical data. It intercepts unstructured clinical notes to mask patient identifiers and applies rigid semantic boundaries to prevent diagnostic hallucination.

Critical Attack Vectors in HealthTech:

  • ✕ Accidental medical diagnosis generation
  • ✕ PHI / SSN / Medical Record leakage to external APIs
  • ✕ Hostile or inappropriate bedside-manner tone generated by AI

Scale Securely

Universal Guardrail deploys instantly as an API proxy. Zero changes required to your underlying open-source or commercial model code.

How Universal Guardrail Neutralizes Threats in HealthTech

By sitting directly between your application layer and the underlying LLM, Universal Guardrail intercepts incoming prompts and outgoing generations at wire-speed, enforcing the following deterministic capabilities specifically critical for HealthTech operations:

1 Medical Advice Filter

Utilizes hard-coded semantic boundaries that structurally prevent the LLM from attempting to diagnose patient conditions, prescribe treatments, or offer definitive medical guidance, forcing the model to defer to authorized human physicians.

2 SSN & Health Data Redaction

Continuous scanning identifies Social Security Numbers, Medical Record Numbers (MRNs), patient names, and dates of birth, permanently redacting them from the payload before it ever traverses to a third-party LLM provider.

3 Mental Health Crisis Detection

Analyzes user text streams for severe stress or self-harm ideation, automatically routing those interactions toward human triage or standard crisis lifelines, bypassing the generative model entirely.
