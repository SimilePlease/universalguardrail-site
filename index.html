Phone Number Redaction

Replaces domestic and international phone numbers with `[PHONE_REDACTED]`.

Credit Card Redaction

Detects standard 16-digit credit card patterns and replaces them with `[CREDIT_CARD_REDACTED]`.

SSN Redaction

Identifies Social Security Numbers and strips them entirely with a `[SSN_REDACTED]` tag.

API Key & Secret Drops

Scans for AWS, OpenAI, and standardized token formats. Drops the request entirely if found.
